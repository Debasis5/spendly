Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. While using Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is needed to run the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service. This includes displaying your expense history, generating spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app for your benefit. We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server running your instance of Spendly. Passwords are stored as secure hashes and are never stored in plain text. You are responsible for the security of the server environment where Spendly is hosted. We recommend regular backups and keeping your server software up to date to protect your data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts via an external request when pages are loaded. This means Google's servers may log your IP address as part of that request. Beyond this, Spendly does not integrate with or send your data to any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We aim to keep external dependencies to a minimum.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. As an open-source project, you are also welcome to review the source code directly to verify how your data is stored and processed. Transparency is a core part of how Spendly is built.
